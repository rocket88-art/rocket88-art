How to play
  1. Flying it up (optional): press Space for full power, then hold ↑ to lift off once you're fast enough. ↑/↓ tilt the nose to climb or dive, and ←/→ turn around. Climb too steeply and you'll stall, so push the nose down to recover. In the balloon, hold ↑ to fire the burner. You can jump from anywhere above 3,500 ft.
  2. Exit: wait for the green light, then press Space to jump. Wind pushes you around, so the best exit point is upwind of the target.
  3. Freefall: ←/→ to track sideways, ↑ backflip, ↓ front flip (tap the same key again mid-flip for a double), Q/E spins, R barrel roll. Hold F to dive head-down: you fall faster and score points every second. Start the next trick right after the last one to build a combo.
  4. Pull: press Space around 3,000 ft. Pull in the middle of a trick and you'll get line twists, or pull while head-down for a hard opening. Forget to pull and the AAD saves you, for a penalty.
  5. Wingsuit: ↑ flares (nose up: slower fall, but you lose speed), ↓ dives (nose down: faster), ←/→ turn around. Flare too long and you'll stall. Flare before you pull, or it's a hard opening.
  6. Ring course: fly through the gold rings in order. Every ring in a row raises your streak. An arrow at the edge of the screen points to the next ring.
  7. Formation: fly into the glowing slot next to your friends and match their speed to dock. Dive (F) to get down to them; tracking slows your fall a little. At 5,500 ft, break off: track away so nobody is close when you pull.
  8. Canopy: ←/→ to steer. Hold ↓ for brakes. Flare (hold ↓) just before touchdown for a stand-up landing. Landing into the wind earns a bonus. For a swoop, turn around between 400 and 150 ft, then flare. Never turn below 150 ft: that's a hard landing.

Every jump earns coins to spend in the gear shop. M mutes the sound. Esc returns to this menu. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons.
