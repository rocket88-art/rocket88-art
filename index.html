How to play
  1. Exit: wait for the green light, then press Space to jump. Wind pushes you around, so the best exit point is upwind of the target.
  2. Freefall: ←/→ to track sideways, ↑ backflip, ↓ front flip, Q/E spins. Start the next trick right after the last one to build a combo.
  3. Pull: press Space around 3,000 ft. Pull in the middle of a trick and you'll get line twists. Forget to pull and the AAD saves you, for a penalty.
  4. Canopy: ←/→ to steer. Hold ↓ for brakes. Flare (hold ↓) just before touchdown for a stand-up landing. Landing into the wind earns a bonus.

Esc returns to this menu. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons.
